HOW CONNECTED MONITORING WORKS

From the pipeline
to your screen.

WEGoT Aqua uses a connected architecture to collect water-use readings from sensors and make the information available through cloud-based interfaces.

01Ultrasonic sensorsInstalled on branch lines / plumbing verticals to capture water-use data.
02LoRa duct unitCollects readings from sensors connected in a series loop.
03LoRa gatewayForwards collected data through the configured 3G/LTE connection.
04Cloud platformProcesses and stores data for access through connected interfaces.
05Web & mobile appHelps residents and facility teams review usage and system alerts.
