What a piezometer measures

A piezometer provides readings associated with groundwater pressure or hydraulic head at the installed sensor location. The correct instrument depends on the purpose of monitoring, the installation depth, the ground conditions and the required reading method.

Piezometer readings can support groundwater observation, engineering monitoring and the review of changing subsurface water conditions. Interpretation of readings should be carried out using the project's technical method and qualified professional judgement.

Piezometer selection checklist

Before selecting an instrument, identify the monitoring purpose and collect as much site information as possible.

  • Required measuring range and installation depth
  • Sensor type and intended monitoring application
  • Borehole details and installation arrangement
  • Cable length, protection and logger compatibility
  • Manual reading or automatic data-logging requirement
  • Communication, telemetry and reporting needs
  • Calibration documentation and project specification requirements

Installation, readings and records

Installation details affect the usefulness of piezometer readings. Keep the borehole log, installation depth, sensor serial number, cable details, commissioning information and baseline reading together in the project record.

For automatic monitoring, confirm the logger, power supply, data interval, communication method and storage or dashboard requirements as one system. Equipment compatibility should be checked before finalizing the configuration.

Service and calibration coordination

For an existing piezometer, share the instrument make or model, serial number, observed fault, display or reading photograph and site location. Calibration or repair requirements should be checked against the instrument type, scope of service and applicable authorization or accreditation documentation before the work is agreed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a piezometer the same as a water-level recorder?

No. A piezometer measures pressure or hydraulic head at its sensor location. A water-level recorder is used to record water-level changes; the correct instrument depends on the monitoring objective.

Can a piezometer be connected to a data logger?

Some piezometer configurations can be paired with compatible data loggers. Confirm sensor output, logger input, cable and communication requirements.

What should I share for a repair enquiry?

Share the serial number, instrument details, site location, issue description and clear photographs of the display or instrument where available.
