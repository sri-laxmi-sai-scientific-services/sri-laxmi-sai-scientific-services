Our Company

Sri Laxmi Sai Scientific Services provides products and services related to groundwater, hydrogeology, water management, scientific instrumentation and field requirements.

Our activities include water metering, groundwater monitoring, water-level measurement, flow-meter support, groundwater auditing, borewell-related services and other technical requirements.

We aim to support customers with suitable products, technical coordination and practical field services for water-management applications.
