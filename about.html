About Our Company

Water Measurement & Groundwater Solutions

Sri Laxmi Sai Scientific Services is a Hyderabad-based scientific equipment and water-management services business. We support customer requirements for water measurement, groundwater monitoring instruments, technical documentation and service coordination.

Who We Are

Practical Support for Scientific Equipment Requirements

We work with customers to understand their application, site conditions and technical requirements before helping identify suitable measurement and monitoring equipment.

Our activities include equipment supply, product enquiries, installation coordination, service requests and supporting documentation for water-management and groundwater-related applications.

We aim to provide clear communication, practical coordination and responsive support throughout the enquiry and service process.

How We Support Customers

  • Understanding application and site requirements
  • Helping identify suitable equipment categories
  • Coordinating product enquiries and quotations
  • Supporting installation and technical-service requests
  • Providing available product and company documentation
  • Coordinating follow-up for service requirements
