Helpful Information

What should you provide?

The more information you provide, the easier it is for us to understand the service requirement.

Instrument Name Tell us whether it is a flow meter, piezometer, DWLR or another instrument.
Serial / FM Number Provide the instrument serial number, FM number or other identification where available.
Issue Description Describe the problem, error, communication issue, physical damage or service requirement.
Site Location Mention the city, state and project location where the service is required.
Photograph / Document Attach a photograph, quotation, report, specification or other supporting document where useful.
Calibration Requirement For calibration requests, mention the instrument type and required calibration service.
