Service Process

How to Request Our Services

Providing the right information helps us understand the requirement and coordinate the next steps.

01

Describe the Requirement

Tell us the type of service, instrument or groundwater-related work you need.

02

Share Site Details

Provide the site location and any available instrument identification details.

03

Attach Documents

Where relevant, include photographs, reports, specifications or other supporting documents.

04

Coordinate Next Steps

Our team can review the submitted details and follow up about the service requirement.
